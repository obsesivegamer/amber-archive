Amber Verification Bridge

The verification council funded a new bridge over the river after a decade of debate about traffic, cost, and the view from the south bank.

Opponents said the money should have gone to buses and safer crossings near the school. Supporters answered that freight already clogs the old span every morning, and that another winter of patching the deck would cost more than starting over.

The mayor called the vote a compromise. Work crews will close one lane of River Street while they set the piers. Local shops asked for weekend access and a printed map of detours. The council promised both, plus a public meeting before the first pour.

Casey Prover reported from the riverwalk, where residents watched survey flags go into the mud and argued about whether the new railings would block the sunset.
